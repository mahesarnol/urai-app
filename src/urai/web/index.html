      Privacy

      Urai App doesn’t collect any data and has no server. Your documents only leave this computer when you use AI mode or AI summary (sent to the AI provider you chose), add a YouTube or web page link (fetched from that site), or convert a voice recording (sent to Google’s speech recognition service).
